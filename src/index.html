Privacy

Your DMs, end-to-end encrypted

Direct messages are encrypted on your devices, so only the people in the conversation can read them. Keys are generated and kept on your devices, and messages can disappear on your schedule.

  • Ephemeral messages that are not kept on our servers
  • Peer-to-peer delivery when both sides are online
  • Your keys never leave your devices
